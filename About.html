MEET THE FOUNDER

Hi, I'm Tani.

My name is Tanika Brown, but most people know me as Tani. I created Blend With Tani with the idea that a smoothie can be more than just a drink — it can be a moment to slow down, refresh, and enjoy something made with care.

I love bringing together fresh fruits, vegetables, and exciting flavors to create blends that feel both delicious and refreshing. Every smoothie is inspired by my love for creativity, freshness, and making people smile.

Blend With Tani is my way of sharing that feeling with everyone — one colorful blend at a time.

— Tanika "Tani" Brown
